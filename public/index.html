How does OpenServ SERV Reasoning run?

Requests to /api/concierge/evaluate call inference-api.openserv.ai/v1 with gpt-5.4-mini, attaching serv_prompt_guard and serv_shadow_agent before Kite caps execute.

Are Kite Mainnet and Testnet both live?

Yes. Protected endpoints advertise eip155:2366 (USDC.e) and eip155:2368 (pieUSD) inside the PAYMENT-REQUIRED header alongside in-browser EIP-3009 signing.

Why route Indonesian bookings to QRIS?

Under Indonesian Currency Law No. 7/2011 Article 21(2)(c), domestic retail settlement uses Rupiah. Cross-border travelers settle in x402 USDC.e while Indonesian residents route straight to IDR QRIS.
