01 Problem Bali High-Season Hospitality

Why booking Bali breaks autonomous agents

WhatsApp

Manual transfer friction

Cliffside beach clubs, villas, and fastboats still lock beds via WhatsApp invoices and bank wires with slow cross-border FX checks.

Prompt Risk

Unbounded wallet danger

Handing a raw wallet to an LLM agent means a single hostile prompt override in venue text can redirect or drain the full balance.

UU 7/2011

Indonesia currency rules

Domestic Indonesian retail transactions require Rupiah (IDR QRIS), whereas cross-border traveler settlements require clean statutory separation.

02 Architecture Separate Reasoning from Financial Authority

Three independent layers protect every dollar

1. OpenServ SERV Reasoning

Evaluates natural-language booking requests via inference-api.openserv.ai/v1 with serv_prompt_guard and serv_shadow_agent enabled.

2. Deterministic Policy Gate

Clamps venue IDs and prices against SQLite inventory truth, checks party capacity, rejects sold-out units, and enforces UU No. 7/2011 rails.

3. Kite Passport & x402

Enforces passkey session allowances ($80/tx, $150/session) and emits real HTTP 402 challenges settled through Pieverse Facilitator v2.

Zero LLM authority over payTo address or settlement price Slide 3 / 8
